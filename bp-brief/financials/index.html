How the cost evolves

Bucket 02 falls as BP capability grows.

The Year 1 baseline is the starting point, not the steady state. As BP teams take operational ownership through the four phases described in the Process page, Valtech's share of the standing delivery team shrinks — and the annual cost falls with it.

Notes & disclaimers

These figures are directional and non-binding, as the RFI requires.

  • All figures excluding VAT, travel, third-party licenses, hosting, and Contentful subscription
  • Standard FTE basis: 40 hours per week, 52 weeks per year (2,080 hours)
  • Engagement percentages reflect average annual effort, not peak workload
  • Year 1 of Bucket 02 reflects ramp-up; subsequent years flex with the trajectory shown above
  • Team composition will be confirmed at RFP stage based on final scope and timeline
  • Hourly rates: 1,450 SEK for Delivery Partner, Contentful Engineers, and React Engineers; 1,700 SEK for all other roles
