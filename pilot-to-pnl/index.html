The problem we keep watching

Corporate innovation moves in quarters. AI moves in weeks.

Enterprises don't lack AI ambition. They lack a way to act on it. Initiatives stall waiting for a "proper data foundation" to be in place. Pilots that prove value never get past the procurement and compliance gauntlet. And when something does ship, attribution to the P&L is so diluted across functions that no one can claim it worked.

95%
of enterprise GenAI investment is delivering zero measurable P&L impact — not because the technology fails, but because the operating environment doesn't let it succeed. Source: MIT NANDA, State of AI in Business 2025.

The answer isn't more AI strategy decks. Enterprises need a place where AI initiatives can move at the pace they actually require — outside the constraints of core operations, compliance and IT platforms — without abandoning the rigour that gets them through to scale.

Waiting for a full data foundation transformation before reaping AI value is itself the most expensive choice. By the time the foundation is ready, the window has moved.

The model

A faster, smaller, less-encumbered version of your organization.

Running on your behalf. Built around your initiatives. Owned by you.

AI Strategy Lab is a managed program inside your business. Each initiative is run as its own branded venture — a small, dedicated team with defined milestones, gate decisions, and KPIs calibrated to the nature of that specific initiative.

The structure imports the discipline of a venture portfolio. A client-owned investment board allocates and reallocates resources across a lean portfolio of three to five initiatives. Each progresses gate by gate, or gets parked — capital freed, learning retained. Initiatives that cross all gates land back inside the core organization with proven economics and P&L attribution intact.

Valtech runs the program. You own everything we build.

How it works

Four stages. Three gates. One decision rule.

Each initiative moves through four stages, with a gate decision at every transition: proceed, refine, or park. Decisions are made by your investment board against milestone-specific KPIs — process measures early, leading indicators mid-cycle, lagging P&L indicators only at the scale gate.

Park is not failure. It's freed capital and retained learning, kept available to the portfolio.

Forward path (proceed) Park (freed capital, retained learning) Gate decision by investment board

Why it works

Three outcomes the standard AI program can't deliver.

Mitigate risk

You commit small, learn fast, and kill discipline is built into the model — not retrofitted after the fact. Tiered funding means no single initiative carries existential weight. The portfolio absorbs failure so the program survives it.

  • Stage-gated funding
  • Kill discipline at every gate
  • Sandbox boundaries pre-negotiated
  • Lean portfolio: three to five initiatives

Move fast

Initiatives operate inside a pre-negotiated regulatory sandbox with explicit carve-outs for data, customer exposure and decision authority. Teams draw on Valtech's global SME bench — no procurement cycles, no vendor onboarding when a specialist is needed for two weeks.

  • Regulatory sandbox with explicit carve-outs
  • Flexible access to Valtech's SME bench
  • Dedicated co-staffed teams
  • No client-side procurement overhead

Scale what wouldn't otherwise scale

The Lab is built to make AI initiatives possible despite the constraints of core enterprise operations, compliance and IT platforms — not after they're resolved. Shared platform assets compound across initiatives; each one starts further along than the last.

  • Designed around core-system constraints
  • Shared platform assets compound
  • Lab speed, not committee speed
  • P&L attribution preserved through branding

Delivery

What an engagement looks like.

Commercial model

A fixed fee covers Valtech's continuous core team — the people who run the Lab, support the investment board, and steward the shared method and platform assets across the portfolio. Specialist resources are then engaged flexibly after each gate decision, scaled up or released to match what the next stage of work requires. Cost adjusts with every board decision rather than running ahead of it.

Governance

A client-owned investment board, convened on a regular cadence to take gate decisions. Valtech runs the program operationally; the client holds decision rights.

Talent commitment

Client team members are engaged at 50% of their time on a given initiative, with a written reversion-and-acceleration guarantee from their sponsor. Their original role is preserved; time on the initiative counts toward career progression.

What you keep

All product IP — every solution built belongs to you. The format, methodology and reusable platform assets stay with Valtech, available to all initiatives in the program.
